Frequently asked questions

Do you offer worldwide delivery?

Yes. We currently ship to selected international destinations, and shipping timelines are provided at checkout.

How long does delivery take?

Local orders typically arrive within 3 to 5 business days, while international orders may take slightly longer depending on the destination.

Can I return an item?

Yes, unworn items in their original condition can be returned within 14 days of delivery for an exchange or refund.

Do you restock popular items?

Some popular styles return seasonally. We recommend checking the shop regularly or contacting us for availability.
